## Invariants & Guardrails

- [ ] **Jaynesian Independence Invariant**: $P(A \land B \mid K) = P(A \mid K) \cdot P(B \mid K)$.
- [ ] **Cross-Product Normalization**: $\sum P(s_a, s_b) = 1.0 \pm 10^{-6}$.
- [ ] **Constraint Isolation**: Linked propositions flagged as dependent.
